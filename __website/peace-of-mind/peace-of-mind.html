Peace of Mind

What is "Peace of Mind"

Peace of mind refers to the sense of peace that comes from having safety nets in your life.

Whether this be from having a financial cushion, healthy body, secure housing, or a self-hosted food system, you need to feel safe.

This page depics different levels of safety nets that contribute to someone's sense of peace. The safety nets are described below, ranked in ascending order of strength:

  1. Bullet-proof

    2. A bullet-proof life means someone can just live securely within the systems around them. This means preventing personal disasters from ruining their life (e.g. managing to afford housing and utilities, preventing health issues)

  2. Self-sustaining

    3. Built on top of a bullet-proof life, a self-sustaining life means someone can start to abstract parts of their life away from other sources and bring control back into their life (e.g. self-sustaining food systems, renewable energy)

  3. Well-protected

    4. A bullet-proof and self-sustaining life detail how individuals can provide safety nets for themselves within the systems they live in. A well-protected life details how the systems themselves can provide safety nets to the people living in them.
