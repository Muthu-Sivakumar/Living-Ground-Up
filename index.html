Welcome!

What is "Living Ground Up"?

I made Living Ground Up to share my views on what constitutes healthy living and build a community that can engage with this topic.

Overall, this site is split between two ideas: how people can achieve peace of mind and genuine joy.

Peace of Mind

In my view, people's default state is contentment. When life's problems bare their fangs, you move from a state of contentment to a state of panic/fear/anxiety/etc. If these problems become long-term, your life is tainted with negative undertones. You may enjoy yourself temporarily, but when those positive feelings are gone you are return to that negative base state.

I think there is a place for philosophy/religion/life perspective in dealing with life's problems, but I honestly can't say whether they give true peace. I s/1ee them as ways to understand the world better, but at the end of the day a problem is still a problem - the only way to 100% get rid of a problem is to fix it.

That is why I believe safety nets are so important to overall well-being.

A safety net means you are aren't living precariously and can stand on your own feet without someone else's help. It means stability and disaster protection.

Genuine Joy

From my very limited life of 22 years, I think joy comes from a few items. multiple levels of social connections, cultivating the body/mind/spirit, dedication to a craft, spontaneity, and an appreciation of the natural world.

While these ideas may seem arbitrarily chosen and the list not exhaustive, I can remember these moments in my life characterized by immense joy.

I believe this kind of genuine joy is the next natural step once someone has peace of mind

How I started this site

Not too long ago I was developing a website called "Solar Media", named as a play of the growing "Solarpunk" movement. The website was designed to foster a community of Solarpunk enthusiasts and share how we can reach that vision. As a quick brief, "Solarpunk" can be loosely defined as a vision for future that centers around using sustainable technology, living in harmony with nature, and stronger communities.

It should also be noted that there isn't a single formal definition of Solarpunk. In fact, I believe a lot of Solarpunk's value isn't in the specifics of what it stands for but rather how it motivates people to push for a future they want to see. I encourage you to check it out for yourself - you may like what you find!

As I coded Solar Media's website I realized I didn't care so much about Solarpunk. Instead, I cared about how Solarpunk's ideas could protect and bring joy in people's lives.

Understanding this, I am developing this site to focus my efforts towards what I truly care about - peace of mind and genuine joy.


Thanks for visiting, and I hope you find your time here meaningful!
